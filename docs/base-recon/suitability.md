- part of the rim is **occluded** by the hand.

It qualifies under *Conditional*:

- the macro shape (a disc) is unambiguous,
- the fine surface detail (growth rings, saw scoring) is exactly the kind that is represented
  procedurally rather than copied,
- the target is a 0.4-unit prop that renders at roughly 24 px on screen, so stylization is
  accepted by definition.

face is reconstructed; the slice's thickness is not observable and is set by the game, not by
the reference.
